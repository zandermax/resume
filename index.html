Professional Summary

I'm a design-focused Software Engineer with 8+ years of experience building product UIs, design systems, and end-to-end features in React, TypeScript, and Node. I like working in the space where product, design, and engineering combine. At Ashby this would look something like taking a problem brief, talking to users, sketching flows, iterating in Figma or low-fidelity prototypes, and then turning those into polished, composable components in code.

Across roles at CHECK24, Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, and earlier-stage companies, I've led front-end designs and implementations for systems used by thousands of internal and external users. My work ranges from micro-frontends and shared component libraries to interaction design, information hierarchy, and accessibility improvements that materially change how fast and confidently people can work.

I'm applying to Ashby because I'm looking for a place where engineers are trusted to own the full loop, including understanding customer problems, writing product specs, wireframing solutions, and implementing them end-to-end in a shared design system. I would thrive in the environment described at Ashby: minimal process, deep ownership, and a strong focus on high-quality tools for working professionals.

Why Ashby & This Role

  • Design + build, together. I've always gravitated toward roles where I can both design and implement software. Ashby's model, in which engineers run projects, write specs, wireframe flows, and then ship production code aligns well with my experience and aspirations.
  • Design systems as leverage. I'm excited by the idea of contributing to (and evolving) an in-house design system used across complex surfaces. I am driven by the idea of building composable components that unlock faster, more consistent experiences for both developers and end-users.
  • Tools for power users. You're building software for teams that live in your product all day. I've repeatedly built UIs for heavy internal and enterprise users, where information density, parsability, and good defaults matter as much as aesthetics. The idea of "approachable but customizable" resonates with what I've tried to do in past roles.
  • Small, talented team with high ownership. Ashby's engineering culture of minimal process, deliberate communication, and a bias toward written proposals and prototypes matches my preference for deep focus time and collaborative, thoughtful critique instead of ceremony.
  • Mission: better tools for working professionals. I like building software that is meaningfully useful. Helping recruiters and talent teams do more with less, with tools that feel as thoughtfully designed as great engineering tooling aligns with the kind of impact I want to have.
